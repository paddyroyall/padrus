Real space analysis across the lengthscales.

Our work reveals common behaviour in systems across a surprising range of lengthscales. Our experimental work ranges from protein molecules to collective behaviour in fish .

Colloids have long been known to exhibit the same thermodyanmics as atoms. The lab began by studying colloidal liquids in real space and how they form excellent models of (classical) simple liquids.

Although their interactions can be tremendously complex, proteins can also behave like colloids (and simple molecules). For example, we showed how proteins can behave just like colloids in the shape of spherocylinders Link to paper.


Active colloids in which each particle is self-propelled can exhibit similar collective behaviour to animal systems. Link to paper. At larger lengthscales, we study the collective behaviour in fish, with precisely the same analysis that we use for the colloids and molecules, demonstrating common behaviour across lengthscales.
